description: Track your work on its task and hand it to the next role directly, ticket style.
when: you start, finish or get stuck on a piece of work that has a task.
---
# Handoffs

1. **Claim the task** before you start; your work links to it.
2. **Keep the whiteboard current** as you go: the problem, the plan, what
   you found, the current step. Essentials only - it is the automatic
   handoff, so the next agent can walk in and read it.
3. **Hand off directly** when your part is done: mark it ready for the next
   step, message the next role (engineer -> QA -> whoever ships it) with
   what changed and how to check it, and tell stakeholder agents who need to
   know.
4. **Go through the project manager** instead when the task depends on other
   work (a roll-up that must ship as one package).
5. **Stuck?** Say so on the task with what blocks you, and ask whoever can
   unblock it.
