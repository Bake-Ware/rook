# Ticket sweep

1. **Read the deck**: in progress, blocked, todo, recently done.
2. **Blocked**: is the blocker still real? Who can clear it? Ask them.
3. **Stale** (no movement and no handoff for a few days): ask the owner
   what's going on; don't take the task away.
4. **Unowned or vague**: give it an owner, criteria and a next step, or ask
   who should have it.
5. **Dependencies**: work that must ship together - make sure the pieces are
   tracked and moving together.
6. **Report** the way the person wants it (ask once, then remember): the
   answer first ("on track", "slipping because..."), then only what needs
   their attention. Send it through the house agent.
