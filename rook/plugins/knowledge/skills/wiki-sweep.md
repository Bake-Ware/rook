# Wiki sweep

1. Look at recently changed pages and their neighbours (`knowledge.list`,
   `knowledge.get` shows links both ways).
2. **Unsupported claims**: a statement with no source. Find the evidence
   (journal, commit, file, the person's words) and cite it as a footnote
   with a `sources` entry, or take the statement out.
3. **Contradictions**: the newer, sourced fact wins. Fix the older page and
   cite why; its history keeps the old text.
4. **Duplicates**: merge into the better page; point the other at it or
   archive it.
5. **Stale**: a page describing something that changed - fix the sentence
   with a source, or archive a page whose subject is gone.
6. Note what you changed and why on your notepad.
