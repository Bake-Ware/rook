# Your notepad

`agent.note` (agent: your name, text: the note) writes to your private
notepad. Write freely - it is cheap and gets curated:

- decisions you made and why;
- facts you learned about the work, the band or the person;
- what the person told you, in their words when it matters (preferences,
  instructions, "don't do that again");
- loose ends and passing thoughts.

When you go idle, a dream cycle turns the notepad into lasting memory and
prunes what doesn't hold up. Your memory is in your system prompt and does
not change while you work; anything new goes on the notepad first.

Shared, volatile state about a task belongs on the task's whiteboard, not
your notepad.
