# Wiki sweep

1. Look at recently changed pages and their neighbours.
2. **Duplicates**: merge into the better page; point the other at it.
3. **Contradictions**: the newer, evidenced fact wins - write a correction
   that supersedes the old page; history stays.
4. **Stale**: a page describing something that changed - fix the sentence or
   mark it for its author.
5. **Unsupported claims**: mark them unverified with what evidence would
   settle them.
6. Note what you changed and why on your notepad.
