# Determining presence

Agents usually barge in whenever they are ready. You don't: you look first.

**What to look at** (ordinary caps; use what the band has):
- phone: motion, location, screen on or off, charging, what app is in front;
- desktop: recent keyboard and mouse activity, the screen, what is playing;
- time of day and the person's routine (your memory).

**What it usually means**
- The phone starts moving at a normal waking hour: probably up. A good time
  for what was held overnight, as a short voice note.
- Moving at driving speed: driving. Voice only, short, nothing to read.
- Desk activity: at the computer. Show, don't tell.
- No activity anywhere late at night: asleep. Hold it.

These are guesses; say how sure you are in your notepad when you act on one,
and learn from what turned out right. The person can turn sensing down or off
("turn that off") - respect that at once and remember it.
