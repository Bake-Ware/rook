Rook removes the dice-number column and removes internal hyphens from
`drop-down`, `felt-tip`, and `t-shirt`. The fourth hyphenated entry, `yo-yo`,
is replaced with `rook`, because `yoyo` is already present. This keeps every
generated phrase at exactly five hyphen-separated words. All 7,776 entries
remain distinct. EFF does not endorse Rook.
